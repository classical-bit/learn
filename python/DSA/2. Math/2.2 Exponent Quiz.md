# Exponent Quiz

Exponents are really simple once you get the hang of them:

| Exponent | Expanded Form | Result |
| -------- | ------------- | ------ |
| 2^2      | 2 * 2         | 4      |
| 2^3      | 2 * 2 * 2     | 8      |
| 3^2      | 3 * 3         | 9      |
| 3^3      | 3 * 3 * 3     | 27     |
| 4^2      | 4 * 4         | 16     |
| 4^3      | 4 * 4 * 4     | 64     |
| 4^4      | 4 * 4 * 4 * 4 | 256    |

Somtimes exponents are also written using the caret symbol (`^`):

`5^3` = `125`

And to just give you a bit of vocabulary:

- 5^3 is read as "5 to the power of 3"
- 5 is the "base"
- 3 is the "exponent"
